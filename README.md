# Health Leveling

**Earn your screen time with your health.** Complete healthy habits and challenges → earn coins → spend coins to unlock your apps. No coins? The apps stay locked.

Android app built with Flutter. Concept validated against Unrot (iOS) with differentiation: automatic verification via Health Connect, structured multi-day challenges, and a granular "small start" coin economy.

Flutter · Health Connect · AccessibilityService (app locking) · local-first storage (sync-ready)

## Monetization

Freemium — first challenge free, all challenges premium (weekly/annual IAP).
